# 🎭 What is Mask Influences?

![Mask Influences demo](/img/AfterFX_kE3rXF7W9e.gif)

- A powerful After Effects extension that enables **precise control of shape layers using masks**, similar to bones or puppet pins — but preserving the power and properties of vectors.

- With fine-tuned influence controls, you can create a more sophisticated and flexible rig setup by **controlling all your shape points with just a few mask points**.
