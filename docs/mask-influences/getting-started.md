# Getting Started

## 1. Set Your Masks

- Select the **Pen tool** from the toolbar.  
  Make sure **"Mask"** is selected in the `tool options` at the top of the interface.

![Pen tool with Mask mode selected](/img/AfterFX_SxcDnLhYwS.gif)

- Select your **shape layer** in the composition.

- Draw your desired bone structure directly on top of your shape using the `pen tool`.  
  For a new mask, **re-click** on your shape layer.  
  This will act as a control system — masks will behave like bones or puppet pins.

![Drawing the bone structure](/img/AfterFX_ztN2FLXZfp.gif)

- Click on the **Mask Influence button** to import your asset:

![Clicking Mask Influence button](/img/AfterFX_kE3rXF7W9e.gif)
